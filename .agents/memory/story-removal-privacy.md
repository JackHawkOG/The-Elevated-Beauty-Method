---
name: Story removal privacy
description: Why member removal claims hide stories before owner verification
---

Member claims of withdrawn permission should hide the affected public story immediately, then remain available for private owner review. A claimant's account identity and explanation are retained only in the owner view, not the public feed.

**Why:** The existing stories have approval records but no reliable account-to-story ownership link. Waiting for owner review leaves potentially unconsented material public, while assuming the claimant is verified would overstate what the app knows.

**How to apply:** Preserve the immediate-hide behavior when changing story requests. If account ownership is later established, revisit how false claims are handled without making withdrawn-permission stories public while disputed.
