## What warrants an EBM project update

Escalate a finding when it changes one of the strategic recommendations in the
competitive analysis:

- A new or materially changed price, membership, payment plan, or access period
- A new diagnostic, personalization, community, or instructor-feedback feature
- A new life-stage, age-positive, confidence, visibility, or presence offer
- A major launch, partnership, certification, book, or live-event program
- A repeated customer complaint that EBM can explicitly solve
- A new proof pattern, testimonial format, or lead magnet worth testing

Every escalation should state:

1. What changed
2. Why it matters to an EBM buyer
3. Which report recommendation it affects
4. The smallest useful response EBM should consider

## 30-minute monthly ritual

| Minutes | Task | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Review alert searches and recent press | Product announcements, interviews, partnerships, or launches |
| 5–10 | Check offer and pricing pages | Price, plan name, payment, access-period, or guarantee changes |
| 10–15 | Check education hubs and blogs | New learning paths, lead magnets, events, or curriculum themes |
| 15–20 | Check public proof | New testimonials, review patterns, or repeated complaints |
| 20–25 | Check category alternatives | Shifts in free classes, creator tutorials, or professional training |
| 25–30 | Update this file | Record the change and recommend a specific EBM response |
