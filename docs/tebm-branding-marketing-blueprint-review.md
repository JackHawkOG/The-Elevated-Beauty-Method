# TEBM Branding & Marketing Blueprint Review

## Status and precedence

The uploaded **Branding & Marketing: Mastery Toolkit Blueprint for TEBM** is the project's source of truth for branding and marketing. Its positioning, visual identity, voice pillars, approved vocabulary, forbidden language, and customer-experience principles govern future customer-facing work.

This review does not rewrite the source document. It records errors and ambiguities that should be resolved before affected pricing, booking, or membership details are implemented.

## Material conflicts requiring confirmation

1. **Tier 3 upfront price is malformed in the executive summary.**  
   The text says `49-$599 upfront`; later sections say `$249-$599 upfront`. The likely intended range is `$249-$599`.

2. **Tier 3 pricing presentation is inconsistent.**  
   Some sections describe Tier 3 as `$97/month`, while others describe it as `$249-$599 upfront and $97/month`. The action-plan shorthand `$0 / $97 / $97` omits the upfront charge and could mislead customers.

3. **Membership names vary.**  
   Tier 3 appears as “Premium TEBM,” “The Elevated Beauty Method,” and “The Elevated Beauty Method Premium.” One customer-facing name should be selected and used consistently.

4. **Retainer and deposit rules conflict.**  
   The lead workflow specifies a `20% retainer`, the service list uses `25% deposit` for several bridal services, and other services use fixed `$125` deposits. These are materially different policies.

5. **The bridal deposit amount conflicts with the stated percentage.**  
   The funnel diagram lists Bridal Glam & Hair at `$450 ($90 deposit)`, which equals 20%. The detailed service list says `$450 (25% deposit)`, which equals `$112.50`.

6. **Fixed deposits do not match the stated 20% system.**  
   A `$125` deposit on a `$325` Event Glam & Hair service and a `$400` Microblading service is not 20%. Clarify whether fixed deposits or a universal percentage govern.

7. **Microblading touch-up price is incomplete.**  
   “Microblading Touch-Up: 00” is missing a currency symbol and leading digit(s).

8. **Premium conversion language is ambiguous.**  
   Section 5 refers to converting clients to the `$97/month Premium digital tier`, but both Tier 2 and Tier 3 are listed at `$97/month`. The tier and any upfront price should be named explicitly.

9. **Masterclass ownership needs clarification.**  
   The strategic map mentions “crowdsourced masterclasses,” while the offer sections emphasize exclusive masterclasses with Nikki. Confirm whether outside experts or community contributors are part of the model.

10. **The final section is incomplete.**  
    The document ends immediately after the heading “Business Map Core Identity & Company Foundations: Purpose-Driven Blueprint for TEBM” and one introductory sentence. It also retains a document-generation prompt immediately before that section.

## Editorial and consistency corrections

- Change `$97mo` to `$97/mo`.
- Change `25%deposit` to `25% deposit`.
- Change `followup notes` to `follow-up notes`.
- Consider replacing “explosive organic growth” with a more measured luxury-brand phrase unless the stronger claim is intentional.
- Clarify whether “10 Frequency Words” is meant literally; the approved vocabulary table contains more than ten entries.
- Standardize `Blushing Beauty by Nikki` versus the `BBBN` abbreviation in customer-facing materials.
- Standardize “deposit” versus “retainer.” These terms can have different contractual meanings and should not be interchanged casually.

## Current implementation mismatch to address only when requested

The current EduPortal landing page displays `$29/month` for The Elevated Method and `$49/month` plus `$1,500 one-time Masterclass access` for the premium tier. The new blueprint instead describes `$97/month` for Tier 2 and `$249-$599 upfront plus $97/month` for Tier 3.

No pricing was changed during this review. A confirmed pricing decision should precede customer-facing updates.

## Safe application rule

Use the blueprint directly for visual style, positioning, voice, vocabulary, and customer-experience direction. For pricing, deposits, retainers, tier naming, or masterclass ownership, resolve the conflicts above before publishing or changing transactional experiences.
