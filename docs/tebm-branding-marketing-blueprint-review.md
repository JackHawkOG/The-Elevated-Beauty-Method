# TEBM Branding & Marketing Blueprint Review

## Status and precedence

The uploaded **Branding & Marketing: Mastery Toolkit Blueprint for TEBM** is the project's source of truth for branding and marketing. Its positioning, visual identity, voice pillars, approved vocabulary, forbidden language, and customer-experience principles govern future customer-facing work.

This review does not rewrite the source document. It records errors and ambiguities that should be resolved before affected pricing, booking, or membership details are implemented.

## Confirmed resolutions

- The membership model now has four tiers. The Founding Member Launch membership file supersedes the older three-tier description.
- Remove “crowdsourced masterclasses.” Masterclasses are exclusive to Nikki.
- Every Wedding/Bridal service requires a 25% retainer.
- Every non-Wedding/Bridal service requires a flat $125 retainer.
- Microblading is $400 for the initial service and $200 for touch-ups.

## Material conflicts still requiring confirmation

1. **Tier 2 customer-facing name.**  
   The launch file uses both “The Elevated Method” and “The Beauty Mindset Accelerator.” Confirm whether the second name is an alternate label, a program inside Tier 2, or should be removed.

2. **Tier 3 final price.**  
   Choose between Option A (`$1,500 upfront + $49/month`), Option B (`$249–$599 upfront + $49–$97/month`), and Option C (`$39/month` plus separate `$1,500–$5,000` masterclass packages), or provide a different final price.

3. **Tier 4 final price.**  
   Choose between Option A (`$248–$488 upfront + $48–$88/month`), Option B (`$1,500 upfront + $88/month`), and Option C (`$1,800–$4,800` cohort packages), or provide a different final price.

4. **Tier 2 founding-member launch window.**  
   Confirm the launch start date, end date, and whether the `$24/month` rate remains valid for the member’s lifetime only while membership stays continuously active.

5. **MARI access by tier.**  
   Tier 1 and Tier 2 list “MARI – AI Imaging Processor,” while Tier 3 and Tier 4 list “MARI – AI Video app.” Confirm whether these are two separate products/features and what each tier can use.

6. **The final blueprint section is incomplete.**  
    The document ends immediately after the heading “Business Map Core Identity & Company Foundations: Purpose-Driven Blueprint for TEBM” and one introductory sentence. It also retains a document-generation prompt immediately before that section.

- Change `25%deposit` to `25% deposit`.
- Change `followup notes` to `follow-up notes`.
- Consider replacing “explosive organic growth” with a more measured luxury-brand phrase unless the stronger claim is intentional.

The Founding Member Launch file defines four tiers and sets Tier 2 at `$48/month`, with a `$24/month` founding-member offer. Tier 3 and Tier 4 pricing still require finalization.

Use the Founding Member Launch file for membership inclusions. Resolve the remaining tier naming, pricing, launch-window, and MARI-access questions before publishing or changing membership transactions.
