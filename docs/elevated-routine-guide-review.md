Existing portal and approved membership materials name **Nikki**. Confirm who should be credited before distributing or adapting the PDF; do not silently replace either name.
2. **MARI availability and claims.** Q3 and Q7 say MARI can match foundation and concealer from one photo; the offer promises priority access. Current membership rules make MARI access conditional on its release. Verify the feature is available and substantiated, or revise these promises to conditional language.
3. **Calls to action and capture.** The PDF's “Claim my Founding Member spot” and “Take The Radiant Audit” buttons have no embedded links. The launch note contains a Google Drive link to the guide, but sending that link alone will not collect an email address or trigger the proposed five-part sequence. Confirm actual destination URLs, opt-in consent, and delivery flow before using this as a list builder.
4. **Brand styling and legibility.** The PDF prints the full brand names with `™` immediately after the last word; current customer-facing copy requires `The Elevated Beauty Method ™` and `The Elevated Beauty Experience ™`. Small muted text on the last page, including price-lock and capacity terms, should be checked at normal mobile reading size and increased in contrast if needed.

The PDF itself has not been rewritten. Keep the source file intact and request an approved revised version before publishing it or adding it to the member library.
