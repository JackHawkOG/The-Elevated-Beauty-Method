## Makeup Answers

1. Understanding your face shape \- What are you?   
   1. Oval  
   2. Round  
   3. Square  
   4. Heart  
   5. oblong

2. Best application for your face shape  
   1. Every face shape needs to be highlighted and contoured differently to achieve the best sense of balance and symmetry.

3. Proper skin prepping for makeup application \- Hydrate, Hydrate, Hydrate\!

4. Best way to hydrate face and body  
   1. Apply moisturizer to damp skin, not dry skin.

5. Best shades for your undertone \- Different skin undertones  
   1. Warm  
   2. Cool  
   3. Neutral  
   4. Olive

6. Why color correction is important  
   1. It allows blemishes to be concealed while using less product

7. How to apply makeup to hide blemishes/ acne/ dark circles  
   1. Apply color corrector first then foundation and powder on top for best coverage.  
   2. What’s the best tool for the job?  
      1. Powders need sponge or brush/ creams need a brush

8. How to properly clean makeup brushes and sponges   
   1. depending on the fennel shape on the brush, do back-and-forth or circular motions with warm water and Johnson and Johnson’s baby shampoo once a month. 

9. The proper way to curl eye lashes with an eyelash curler \-   
   1. The bottom portion of the curler should never be resting on your cheek, it needs to be touching your eyelid, it’s all about the angle.

10. Tips for applying false eyelashes  
    1. Depending on a shape angle, fall lashes app while holding with tweezers and keep eye open while glueing. 

11. Why SPF is important; but not for photography  
    1. Titanium dioxide is the main ingredient in all SPF products, it’s designed to reflect light, but this also happens with flash photography, making you appear ghostly and pale because the product is doing its job. Skip it for big events and pictures.

12. Best way to apply eyeshadows for your eye shape  
    1. False eyelashes for every style and occasion

13. Why primers are great for all day wear  
    1. Skin is the largest organ we have and anything applied to it gets absorbed over time. The right kind of primer allows Makeup to stay in place all day so it won’t be absorbed in your skin or wear off with time. 

14. A good way to remove your makeup  
    1. coconut oil around eyes too break up waterproof products then use warm water and Johnson Johnson’s baby shampoo to cleanse the rest. Pat dry, then hydrate\!

15. Best makeup products for traveling

16. Best makeup application for mature skin  
    1. Light cream products and minimal powders to avoid fine lines, big pores, and wrinkles

17. How to go from daytime makeup to nighttime makeup  
    1. Simply adding shimmer to the inner corner of the eye, enhancing contour/blush and an extra pop of color to the lip will take your look from day to night in 5 minutes.

18. The difference between everyday Makeup and camera ready Makeup  
    1. Every day makeup is typically quicker to achieve that enhances facial features. Camera ready makeup requires full face application with proper highlight and contouring application for added dimension. It doesn’t need to be dark, it just needs to be well defined.

19. Tricks for not getting lipstick on your teeth  
    1. After applying, use your thumb and place it in your mouth like a baby and pull out. Any product that’s not where it should be will be removed.

20. Best eyeshadow colors based on your eye color  
    1. Blue Eyes — golds, pinks, peaches  
    2. Green eyes —  purple, mauves  
    3. Brown eyes — universal so most colors work. 

21. My favorite products and why  
    1. Krylon foundations because the silicone formula allows the product to glide on easily for full coverage and feels lightweight all day.

22. How to change the shape and size of your nose  
    1. Flat nose/ strong highlight in the center to bring it out. Wide nose/ strong highlight down center with contour close to highlight on each side. Long nose/ no highlight and contour tip and bottom. Asymmetrical/ strong highlights, and contour to make it appear straighter.

23. Why brows are so important, and how to fix them  
    1. Brows frame the eyes, they can make eyes appear bigger and brings lift to the face when combed upward and filled to define the arch.

24. Different types of smoky eye makeup looks  
    1. Dark smoky  
    2. daytime smoky  
    3. smoky eyeliner

25. Dramatic makeup vs. Soft Makeup  
    1. Drama is created with sharper lines, color definition, and louder pops of color. Soft makeup minimal with darker colors, smudged or smoked out for a light effect.

26. Why I keep two foundation colors in my Makeup bag at all times  
    1. As seasons change, so does your skin color, more tan during the summer, more pale during the winter. Keeping two foundation colors allows you to mix them together, creating the perfect shade for your skin any time of year.

27. How to enhance the color of your eyes using makeup  
    1. applying eyeliner to the bottom waterline will reflect back into the iris especially in daylight enhancing the color of your eye. 

28. Makeup shouldn’t stop at your jawline  
    1. Extend foundation and contour down your neck and sometimes your shoulders depending on the outfit creates a seamless look; and don’t forget your ears.

## Body-Style Answers

1. How to instantly look 10 pounds lighter in a photo  
   1. Know your angels, hips back, shoulders down, long neck and head out and tilted slightly down. 

2. How to sit when taking a photo while sitting  
   1. Sit toward the end of the chair while slightly leaning forward and legs should be slightly bent to the slide to make you appear longer. Pull down shirts and jackets to eliminate large wrinkles in the material.

3. What body shape do you have?   
   1. Hourglass, pear, apple, rectangle, inverted triangle

4. Best way to style for your body shape  
   1. First understand your body shape, and just because it zips and doesn’t mean it fits. 

5. Why fabric matters  
   1. Depending on the season the right kind of fabric will fight against you or Work with you and quality matters. It’s better to invest in a nice article of clothing versus buying something cheap that shows wear and tear over a short time. 

6. Understanding your colors  
   1. The wrong color shade can bring out imperfections in your skin appearing flat or sickly. The right color shade can make your skin look vibrant and make your eyes pop.

7. What colors should you stay away from?   
   1. Understand your Color season and shop for colors in that range

8. How to dress your wage or age.  
   1. Dressing more mature, or modest doesn’t have to be boring

9. Just because it zips, doesn’t mean it fits.  
   1. You zipped it up, but can you sit, bend over or move without readjusting your clothes. No one can see the size on the tag so size up if needed.

10. What you need for business headshot photos.  
    1. A professional Makeup in Hair Stylist, multiple outfit options, some professional and casual options, appropriate jewelry and accessories.

11. If it feels weird, that means you’re doing it right.  
    1. Different angles photograph better than others so listen to your photographer and if it feels weird in person, it usually looks great in the photo. 

12. How to accessorize for head shots.  
    1. Big statement pieces/jewelry can overpower the person. Keep it clean, classy for a timeless look.

13. Best body angles for photography.  
    1. Depending on your body shape, listen to your photographer.

14. How to go from business to night life.  
    1. Classic and timeless styles/ fashion  
    2. Think old money; nice fabrics, no designer labels, beautiful prints, neutral colors.

15. How to master the sexy, sultry look in photos.  
    1. Smile with your eyes while keeping your mouth soft and relaxed with lips slightly parted. 

16. What to do with your arms/hands in photos.  
    1. It depends on the style of the pictures, keep hands and fingers relaxed while keeping fingers together. 

17. Body makeup for bruising and scar camouflage.  
    1. Westmore Beauty Body Makeup is a great waterproof product that disguises bruises, veins and scars while leaving your skin glowy. 

18. Best Tattoo cover up Makeup.  
    1. Krylon air brush formula 

19. How to cover a tattoo using makeup.  
    1. 1\) Remove any hair from the area and clean with alcohol.   
    2. 2\) Apply a thin layer of color corrector-orange/red to soften black and let dry. (Repeat as needed).   
    3. 3\) Apply a thin layer of skin tone color in a lighter shade and let dry. Repeat as needed and the final layer should be color matched to the skin.   
    4. 4\) Set with translucent powder.   
    5. 5\) Spray with a final seal to lock it in place. 

20. How to demand attention through body language.  
    1. Confidence is Key\! Head up, shoulders back. Don’t be afraid to look people in the eye and move with intention.

## Hair-Styling Answers

1. What does it mean to train your hair?   
   1. Sometimes it requires several months up to a year but training your hair and scalp is possible depends on what you’re trying to achieve. Ex. You wash your hair every day because it feels super oily by the end of the day. Hair was not meant to be washed every day, excessive chemicals strip your hair and scalp. The best thing to do is wash your hair only once a week. At first, your hair will be super oily, but you will have to fight through it. As time goes by your scalp will produce less oil since it doesn’t feel the need to replenish. And that is how you train\!

2. My top five hair care products  
   1. Understanding your hair type \- Curly, straight, fine, thick…  
   2. The best way to get volume:  
      1. A styling moose applied at the roots, blowout pulling up and away from the scalp and set with a big velcro roller.   
      2. Remove roller after 20 and use texture powder on roots if needed. 

3. The best tool for the job.  
   1. Tell me about the job and I’ll tell you the tool to use. 

4. My trick for removing small rubber bands.  
   1. Simply use a hot tool like straightener or curling iron, hold it over the rubber band to burn it, and it will come right out. But don’t leave the heat on it too long because the rubber will melt into the hair. 

5. Best trick to hide grays.  
   1. Use old black or brown mascara if you’re in a pinch. Otherwise, L’Oreal root spray works very well.

6. The best way to wash your hair.  
   1. Use mild to cold water, shampoo scalp and roots the rinse. Repeat. Then condition the ends of your hair and come through. Rinse well. Towel dry with a microfiber towel and allow the rest to air dry. 

7. Natural ways to soothe your scalp.  
   1. Coconut oil helps with inflammation and helps fight fungus, which is what dandruff is.  
   2. Saturate your whole scalp with coconut oil the night before you plan on washing it. Sleep with a towel on your pillow because it’ll be messy.   
   3. When you wash, add a few drops of Tea tree oil to your shampoo, your scalp will feel super clean.

8. Why dirty hair styles better than clean hair?  
   1. When hair is freshly washed and has no oil, dirt or products, it makes it challenging for the hair to grip to itself. When hair is either slightly dirty or has a little product filter up it gives the hair more structure.

9. Different types of hairspray and when to use them.  
   1. flexible hairspray still allows you to move it easily with soft hold. Firm spray is used after styling to keep that style from moving if only a little.

10. How to properly tease your hair for volume.  
    1. Texture powder makes this step easier. Section Off an area and pull it straight up away from the scalp, sprinkle texture powder at roots. Take a teasing comb 2 to 3 inches from the scalp and push down creating a little nest. Repeat a few times and hair instantly has more volume.

11. The importance of face framing layers.  
    1. different hair layers around the face can help soften the appearance of a face shape.

12. Is your hair color the right color for you?   
    1. I need a color analyst

13. What do I do with my sideburns?  
    1. Incorporate into your hairstyle or remove by laser, waxing or razor.

14. How to make thin hair look thicker.  
    1. The right products for your hair type and teasing at the root

15. The best type of Hair Extensions that do the least amount of damage.  
    1. Carissa (extension expert) — Different hair extension recommendations…

16. The difference between real human hair extensions and synthetic hair.  
    1. Texture is the biggest difference, but the most important thing is real human hair can take hot tools like curling iron and straightener while synthetic hair will melt under high temperatures making it harder to style.

17. Best way to curl naturally curly hair.  
    1. Follow the natural curl pattern

18. Why humidity affects hair.  
    1. When hair follicles sense moisture they open and expand outward in an attempt to shed dirt and oil. So when it’s humid, the hair is pulling moisture out of the air, causing it to frizz.

19. Does hair color and eyebrow color need to match?   
    1. Not necessarily. When I apply Makeup I tend to give blondes a slightly darker brow color to make them pop. For dark brunettes, I use a soft brown color to diffuse the brows if needed.

20. Best way to secure bobby pins.  
    1. Use 2 pins and crisscross one over the other to lock them together

21. The best way to remove static from your hair.  
    1. use a dryer sheet and run it along hair from top to bottom 

22. Slow and steady when it comes to straightening your hair.  
    1. Running the straighter from root to tip in a slow consistent movement with firm pressure once will straighten your hair better than doing it five times fast.
