**Part 1: Makeup Mastery**:	

* **8 Modules:**  
  * 01 \- Face Shape and Application  
  * 02 \- Skin Preparation and Hydration  
  * 03 \- Skin Undertones and Shades  
  * 04 \- Color Correction and Blemish Camouflage  
  * 05 \- Tools and Cleaning  
  * 06 \- Eyelashes and Eyeshadow Techniques  
  * 07 \- Application Settings and Longevity  
  * 08 \- Special Tips and Mature Skin  
* **28 Topics:**    
  * Face shape contouring, skin hydration prepping, decoding 6 undertones, blemish/scar color correction, lash application angles, day-to-night 5-minute shifts, mature skin techniques, and camera-ready vs. everyday makeup.  
* **Answers and guidance for the Makeup Mastery topics from the curriculum:**  
* Understanding your face shape: Includes Oval, Round, Square, Heart, and Oblong.  
* Best application for your face shape: Every face shape needs to be highlighted and contoured differently to achieve balance and symmetry.  
* Proper skin prepping for makeup application: Hydrate, hydrate, hydrate\! Apply moisturizer to damp skin, not dry.  
* Best shades for your undertone: Categorized into Warm, Cool, Neutral, and Olive undertones.  
* Why color correction is important: Allows blemishes to be concealed while using less product.  
* How to apply makeup to hide blemishes, acne, and dark circles: Apply color corrector first, then foundation and powder on top for best coverage. Powders need a sponge or brush; creams need a brush.  
* How to properly clean makeup brushes and sponges: Depending on the ferrule shape, use back-and-forth or circular motions with warm water and Johnson & Johnson’s baby shampoo once a month.  
* The proper way to curl eyelashes with an eyelash curler: The bottom portion of the curler should touch your eyelid, never resting on your cheek—it's all about the angle.  
* Tips for applying false eyelashes: Hold with tweezers while keeping the eye open during gluing, depending on the shape angle.  
* Why SPF is important, but not for photography: Titanium dioxide in SPF products reflects light, which causes flash photography to make you appear ghostly and pale. Skip it for big events and pictures.  
* Why primers are great for all-day wear: The right kind of primer prevents makeup from being absorbed into the skin over time and keeps it in place all day.  
* The best way to remove your makeup: Use coconut oil around the eyes to break up waterproof products, then use warm water and baby shampoo to cleanse the rest. Pat dry and hydrate.  
* Best makeup application for mature skin: Use light cream products and minimal powders to avoid fine lines, big pores, and wrinkles.  
* How to go from daytime makeup to nighttime makeup: Add shimmer to the inner corner of the eye, enhance contour/blush, and add an extra pop of color to the lip for a 5-minute transition.  
* The difference between everyday makeup and camera-ready makeup: Everyday makeup quickly enhances features, while camera-ready makeup requires full-face application with proper highlight and contouring for well-defined dimension.  
* Tricks for not getting lipstick on your teeth: After applying, place your thumb in your mouth like a baby and pull straight out to remove excess product.  
* Best eyeshadow colors based on your eye color: Blue eyes pair with golds, pinks, and peaches; green eyes with purples and mauves; and brown eyes are universal, so most colors work.  
* My favorite products and why: Krylon foundations because their silicone formula glides on easily for full coverage while feeling lightweight all day.  
* How to change the shape and size of your nose: Use highlights and contours strategically—such as a strong center highlight for a flat nose, highlights down the center with close contours for a wide nose, or contouring the tip and bottom for a long nose.  
* Why brows are so important, and how to fix them: Brows frame the eyes, make them appear bigger, and lift the face when combed upward and filled in to define the arch.  
* Different types of smoky eye makeups: Dark smoky, daytime smoky, and smoky eyeliner.  
* Dramatic makeup vs. soft makeup: Drama is created with sharper lines, color definition, and loud pops of color, whereas soft makeup uses minimal, smudged, or smoked-out darker colors for a light effect.  
* Why I keep two foundation colors in my makeup bag at all times: Skin color changes with the seasons (tan in summer, pale in winter); keeping two colors lets you mix them to create the perfect shade year-round.  
* How to enhance the color of your eyes using makeup: Applying eyeliner to the bottom waterline reflects back into the iris, especially in daylight, to enhance eye color.  
* Makeup shouldn’t stop at your jawline: Extend foundation and contour down your neck, onto your ears, and sometimes your shoulders depending on the outfit to create a seamless look.

**Part 2: Hair-Styling & Care**

* **5 Modules:**  
1. Hair Care and Maintenance Basics  
2. Styling, Teasing, and Volume  
3. Hair Extensions Guide  
4. Coloring and Face Framing  
5. Tools, Tricks, and Environment  
* **22 Topics:**   
  * Including: Scalp/hair training rituals, root volume teasing, extension selection (human vs. synthetic),  gray root coverage, humidity protection, and blemish/hairline framing.  
* **Answers and guidance for the Hair-Styling topics from the curriculum:**  
* **What does it mean to train your hair?** Training your hair and scalp can take several months up to a year depending on your goal. Washing hair every day strips the hair and scalp with excessive chemicals; instead, transition to washing only once a week. Though hair will feel oily initially, over time the scalp produces less oil as it adjusts.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **Understanding your hair type / Getting volume:** Hair types include curly, straight, fine, or thick. To achieve volume, apply styling mousse at the roots, perform a blowout pulling up and away from the scalp, and set with a large velcro roller for 20 minutes. Use texture powder on roots if needed.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **The best tool for the job:** The correct tool depends on the specific job or hair goal being performed.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **My trick for removing small rubber bands:** Hold a hot tool (like a straightener or curling iron) over the rubber band briefly to burn it off. Avoid leaving the heat on too long so the rubber does not melt into the hair.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **Best trick to hide grays:** Use old black or brown mascara in a pinch, or apply L'Oreal root spray.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **The best way to wash your hair:** Wash using mild to cold water, shampoo the scalp and roots, rinse, and repeat. Condition the ends and comb through, rinse well, towel dry with a microfiber towel, and let the rest air dry.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **Natural ways to soothe your scalp:** Coconut oil reduces inflammation and fights fungus (dandruff). Saturate the entire scalp with coconut oil the night before washing and sleep with a towel on your pillow. When washing, add a few drops of tea tree oil to your shampoo for a clean feel.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **Why dirty hair styles better than clean hair:** Freshly washed hair lacks oil, dirt, or product, making it difficult for hair strands to grip each other. Slightly dirty hair or product buildup provides structural grip.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **Different types of hairspray and when to use them:** Flexible hairspray allows movement with a soft hold, while firm hairspray is used after styling to hold the style firmly in place.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **How to properly tease your hair for volume:** Section off hair, pull it straight up away from the scalp, and sprinkle texture powder at the roots. Place a teasing comb 2 to 3 inches from the scalp and push down repeatedly to create a nest for instant volume.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **The importance of face framing layers:** Hair layers around the face help soften face shapes.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **Is your hair color the right color for you?** A color analyst can help determine the best hair color for your features.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **What do I do with my sideburns?** Incorporate sideburns into your hairstyle, or remove them using laser treatment, waxing, or a razor.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **How to make thin hair look thicker:** Use product formulas tailored to your hair type and tease hair at the root.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **The best type of Hair Extensions:** Consult an extension expert (such as Carissa) for specific damage-minimizing recommendations.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **The difference between real human hair extensions and synthetic hair:** Real human hair can handle heat styling tools like curling irons and straighteners, whereas synthetic hair melts under high heat.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **Best way to curl naturally curly hair:** Follow the natural curl pattern of the hair.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **Why humidity affects hair:** When hair follicles sense environmental moisture, they expand outward to attempt to shed dirt and oil, drawing in moisture and causing frizz.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **Does hair color and eyebrow color need to match?** They do not need to match exactly; blonde hair often pairs well with a slightly darker brow to pop, whereas dark brunettes benefit from a soft brown shade to diffuse the brows.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **Best way to secure bobby pins:** Use two bobby pins and crisscross one over the other to lock them together securely.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **The best way to remove static from your hair:** Run a dryer sheet along the hair from top to bottom.[1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZtOtzCGt7_jMm5wXaFULmfZrp1IizoBZCPKi4z59bOo/edit)  
* **Slow and steady when straightening your hair:** Running a straightener from root to tip in a single, slow, consistent movement with firm pressure straightens hair better than five fast passes.

**Part 3: Body-Style & Image/Presence**

* **5 Modules:**  
1. Photography Posing and Angles  
2. Body Shape and Styling Analysis  
3. Wardrobe and Fit Concepts  
4. Professional Headshots  
5. Camouflage and Communication  
* **20 Topics:**   
  * Including: Posing to look 10 lbs lighter, sitting angles, body shape styling, wardrobe fabric selection, headshot preparation, tattoo camouflage, and commanding room presence.  
* **Answers and guidance for the Body-Style & Presence topics from the curriculum:**  
* How to instantly look 10 pounds lighter in a photo: Know your angles, keep your hips back, shoulders down, long neck, and head out and tilted slightly down.  
* How to sit when taking a photo while sitting: Sit toward the end of the chair while slightly leaning forward, with your legs slightly bent to the side to make you appear longer. Pull down shirts and jackets to eliminate large wrinkles in the material.  
* What body shape do you have?: Hourglass, pear, apple, rectangle, and inverted triangle.  
* Best way to style for your body shape: First understand your body shape, and remember that just because it zips doesn’t mean it fits.  
* Why fabric matters: Depending on the season, the right kind of fabric will either work with you or against you, and quality matters. It’s better to invest in a nice article of clothing versus buying something cheap that shows wear and tear quickly.  
* Understanding your colors: The wrong color shade can bring out skin imperfections and make you appear flat or sickly, while the right color shade can make your skin look vibrant and make your eyes pop.  
* What colors should you stay away from?: Understand your color season and shop for colors within that range.  
* How to dress your wage or age: Dressing more maturely or modestly doesn’t have to be boring.  
* Just because it zips, doesn’t mean it fits: Make sure you can sit, bend over, or move without readjusting your clothes. No one can see the size on the tag, so size up if needed.  
* What you need for business headshot photos: A professional makeup and hair stylist, multiple outfit options (including professional and casual choices), and appropriate jewelry and accessories.  
* If it feels weird, that means you’re doing it right: Different angles photograph better than others, so listen to your photographer; if a pose feels weird in person, it usually looks great in the photo.  
* How to accessorize for head shots: Big statement pieces and jewelry can overpower a person. Keep it clean and classy for a timeless look.  
* Best body angles for photography: Depending on your body shape, listen to your photographer's guidance.  
* How to go from business to night life: Opt for classic and timeless styles (think "old money"): nice fabrics, no designer labels, beautiful prints, and neutral colors.  
* How to master the sexy, sultry look in photos: Smile with your eyes while keeping your mouth soft and relaxed with your lips slightly parted.  
* What to do with your arms/hands in photos: It depends on the style of the picture, but keep your hands and fingers relaxed while keeping your fingers together.  
* Body makeup for bruising and scar camouflage: Westmore Beauty Body Makeup is a great waterproof product that disguises bruises, veins, and scars while leaving skin looking glowy.  
* Best Tattoo cover up Makeup: Krylon airbrush formula.  
* How to cover a tattoo using makeup:  
  1. Remove any hair from the area and clean with alcohol.  
  2. Apply a thin layer of orange/red color corrector to soften black ink and let dry (repeat as needed).  
  3. Apply a thin layer of skin-tone color in a lighter shade and let dry (repeat as needed), with the final layer color-matched to the skin.  
  4. Set with translucent powder.  
  5. Spray with a final seal to lock it in place.  
* How to demand attention through body language: Confidence is key\! Keep your head up and shoulders back, don’t be afraid to look people in the eye, and move with intention.
