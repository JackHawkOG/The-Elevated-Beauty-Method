### **Typography Hierarchy**

**Primary Serif Font — Headings & Hero Statements:  Cormorant Garamond**  
An elegant, high-contrast serif font conveying timeless elegance, luxury craftsmanship, and editorial authority.

**Primary Sans-Serif Font — Body & UI Controls:**  **Lato**  
A clean, warm sans-serif providing high legibility across long-form educational guides, button controls, badges, and navigation menus.

### 

### **Interface Component Standards**

**Buttons & Badges:**   
Pill-shaped rounded geometry with soft champagne gold fills (\#FFEBBF) or restrained dark borders.

**Card Structures:**   
Soft black backgrounds (\#141414) with subtle warm dark gray borders (\#2F2E2B) and low-opacity champagne glows on hover.

**App Navigation Layers:**   
Standardized across Onboarding, Dashboard, Learning Library, Course Detail, Community Feed, and Profile views.
